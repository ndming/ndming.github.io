I completed my Master's degree within the distinguished Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's COSI Program at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. As part of the Class of 2025, I graduated with first-class honors and was awarded Best Master's Thesis for my research conducted at Carl Zeiss AG in Germany, under joint supervision with Université Jean Monnet in France. My thesis was supervised by Dr. Malte Avenhaus, Dr. Thomas Lindemeier, and Associate Professor, Dr. Philippe Colantoni.

Before joining COSI, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, graduating with first-class honors and receiving the Gold Medal for outstanding academic performance. My Bachelor's thesis, a multi-platform navigation system using graphics rendering, was supervised by Associate Professor, Dr. Cuong Pham-Quoc, Head of the Computer Engineering Department.
